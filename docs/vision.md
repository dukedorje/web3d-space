# Vision

**Product: [All Systems Go](all-systems-go/VISION.md).** This repo is the ground for a local-first AI camera, assistant director, and spatial filmmaking suite. Start at [docs/all-systems-go/README.md](all-systems-go/README.md).

## Heritage

This tree began as a father–son WebGPU lab (boids, PlayCanvas splat viewer, capture-to-immersive research). That work is the **spatial canvas**: hybrid mesh + Gaussian splat in the browser, dual-branch COLMAP → gsplat + photogrammetry. Sprint notes and research stay under `docs/sprints/` and `docs/research/`. They are not the product identity anymore.
